Laptop rental questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about laptop hire, pricing and quotation requests through DY-PLUS and ATLAS Rentals.

What is the minimum laptop rental quantity?

The minimum rental quantity is 5 laptops.

How much does laptop rental cost?

Standard Business Laptops cost ₦10,000 daily, ₦59,500 weekly or ₦185,000 monthly per laptop. High Performance Laptops cost ₦15,000 daily, ₦89,500 weekly or ₦225,500 monthly per laptop. VAT is 7.5%.

Do you provide laptop delivery and retrieval?

Yes. Delivery and retrieval is included as a standard rental service and charged once at ₦40,000.

Can laptops be rented for training, conferences and examinations?

Yes. Laptops can be requested for training programmes, conferences, examinations and corporate events, subject to availability and DY-PLUS confirmation.

Are technicians available?

Yes. An on-site technician is optional at ₦35,000 per day.

Do you serve Lagos and Abuja?

Yes. Lagos and Abuja are the primary service cities.

How do customers request a quotation?

Complete the four-step rental planner and submit the enquiry. DY-PLUS provides an enquiry reference and quotation; availability and final booking remain subject to confirmation.
